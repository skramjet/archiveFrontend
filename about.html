About IRP

The Institute for Research and Policy (IRP) is an interdisciplinary research collective composed of student researchers, postdoctoral scholars, staff, and faculty in the College of Liberal Arts at The University of Texas at Austin. IRP serves as an interdisciplinary, practice-oriented research institute dedicated to expanding student opportunities while advancing community-engaged research and policy analysis.

This website was developed as a digital archive to serve people interested in exploring the history of Texas and the American South. We developed this archive to help people access Central Texas census data in machine-readable formats, along with curated archival documents about the lives of people in the antebellum South. We hope this provides a fuller historical picture for researchers, students, and community members.

The Data

The Central Texas Database Project is a joint effort among faculty and student researchers at IRP with the goal of creating an open-access demographic database spanning several Central Texas counties using census data from 1850-1950. The Textual Database, a portion of the Central Texas Database Project, is meant to complement the census data by adding archival dimensions to the data.

Our census data is accessible online and free to download in the CSV format. As a whole, it is meant to describe life in historic Central Texas.
